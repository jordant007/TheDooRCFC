Our Story

Building a community of faith, hope, and love since 2010

Who We Are

The Door Christian Fellowship Church is a Bible-believing community dedicated to spreading the Gospel of Jesus Christ. Founded in 2010, we have grown from a small gathering to a vibrant congregation committed to worship, fellowship, and community service.

Our church family welcomes people from all walks of life, creating a diverse and inclusive environment where everyone can experience God's love and grace.
